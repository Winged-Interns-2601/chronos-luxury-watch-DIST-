The Origin

An Obsession with the Second

CHRONOS did not begin in a factory. It began with an uncompromising pursuit to capture the weight of passing hours. We believe a watch is not a tool to measure time—it is a gallery piece worn upon the wrist.

Every gear, hairline spring, and sapphire crystal casing is assembled by hand under high magnification. It takes a master artisan up to three months to anchor a single mechanical timepiece.
