Our House

Built by Hand,
in a Small Geneva Workshop

Meridian was founded in 1962 by a single watchmaker who believed a timepiece should outlive the person who bought it. That belief still shapes every decision we make — from the alloy in our cases to the supplier we choose for a single screw.

Today the atelier is larger, but the process hasn't changed. Every movement is assembled, regulated, and tested by hand before it earns the Meridian mark on its caseback.
